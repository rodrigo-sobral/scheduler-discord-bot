# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

## Overview

Scheduler Discord Bot ("the Bot") respects your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Bot processes, why it is processed, and how long it is retained.

---

## Information Collected

In order to provide scheduling functionality, the Bot stores the following information:

### Discord identifiers

- Discord User ID of the message creator
- Destination Discord User IDs
- Destination Discord Channel IDs

These identifiers are necessary to determine who created a scheduled message and where it should be delivered.

---

### Scheduled message information

The Bot stores:

- message content;
- scheduled delivery date and time;
- recurrence settings (if applicable);
- attachment URL (when a supported attachment is included).

This information is required so the Bot can deliver scheduled messages.

---

### Delivery status

The Bot records operational information including:

- whether a message has been delivered;
- delivery timestamp;
- delivery failures.

---

### Application logs

The Bot generates operational logs for debugging and maintenance purposes.

Logs may contain technical information such as:

- timestamps;
- error messages;
- Discord identifiers;
- delivery status.

Logs are not used for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

---

## Information Not Collected

The Bot does **not** intentionally collect:

- passwords;
- payment information;
- email addresses;
- IP addresses;
- location information;
- Discord private messages unrelated to scheduled deliveries;
- analytics or tracking information.

The Bot does not sell user data.

---

## Purpose of Processing

Information is processed exclusively to:

- schedule messages;
- deliver scheduled messages;
- notify users of delivery success or failure;
- maintain and troubleshoot the Bot.

No automated profiling or marketing activities are performed.

---

## Data Sharing

The Bot does not sell, rent, or disclose your information to third parties.

Data is shared only:

- with Discord as required to send scheduled messages;
- when required by applicable law.

---

## Data Retention

Scheduled messages are retained while they are pending delivery.

Delivered messages and delivery records may remain in the database until they are removed by the Bot or the database is cleaned by the operator.

Operational logs are retained only for maintenance purposes.

---

## Security

Reasonable technical measures are taken to protect stored information.

However, no storage system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

---

## Third-Party Services

The Bot relies on Discord's platform and APIs.

Use of Discord is governed by Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

---

## Your Rights

You may stop using the Bot at any time.

If you wish to request deletion of information associated with your scheduled messages, you may contact the developer.

---

## Children's Privacy

The Bot is not specifically directed toward children.

Users must comply with Discord's minimum age requirements.

---

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically.

The most recent version will always be available in this repository.
